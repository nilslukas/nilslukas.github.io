Guten Tag, I’m Nils 👋. I’m an Assistant Professor of Machine Learning at MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi, where I lead the Secure, Private, Open and Trustworthy  AI lab and co-direct the Center for Secure Agentic Control (CSAC). My group works on watermarks, adaptive attacks, and privacy and security for AI agents.

I did my PhD at the University of Waterloo, where my thesis received the Top Mathematics Doctoral Prize and I received the 2024 Alumni Gold Medal. Since 2025, my research has been awarded nearly $2 million in research grants, including an Amazon Research Award and an AED 1 million award from the MBZUAI Innovation Program. Recently I founded Adherence, a startup delivering enterprise AI agents. Check out our website.
